# Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the direct marketing campaigns (phone calls) of a banking institution. The primary objective is to evaluate the effectiveness of these campaigns and identify the key customer demographics and economic indicators that drive a client to subscribe to a bank term deposit (Target Variable: `y`).

**Author:** Hrusikesh Behera

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Data Analytics & Visualization:** Processed and analyzed using Excel and dynamic dashboarding tools to track campaign success rates.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Extracting patterns from customer demographics, financial standing, and call metrics.

## 📊 Dataset Highlights
The dataset (`Bank Report.csv`) contains rich customer and macroeconomic data, categorized into:
* **Client Demographics:** Age, Job Title, Marital Status, and Education level.
* **Financial Standing:** Credit in default, Housing loan status, and Personal loan status.
* **Campaign Metrics:** Contact communication type (cellular/telephone), call duration, and previous campaign outcomes.
* **Macroeconomic Indicators:** Employment variation rate, consumer price index, and Euribor 3-month rate.

## 💡 Key Objectives & Analysis Areas
1. **Conversion Rate Analysis:** Identifying which demographic groups (e.g., specific job sectors, age brackets, or education levels) yield the highest term deposit subscriptions.
2. **Call Duration Impact:** Evaluating how the length of the contact call (`duration`) correlates with a successful outcome. 
3. **Economic Context:** Understanding how broader economic indicators (like the consumer confidence index) affect a customer's willingness to invest in a term deposit.
4. **Campaign Optimization:** Analyzing the success rate of previous campaigns (`poutcome`) to refine future targeting strategies and reduce unnecessary outreach.
